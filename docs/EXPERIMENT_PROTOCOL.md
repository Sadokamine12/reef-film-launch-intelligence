# EUR 500 experiment protocol

## Goal

Maximize useful learning first, then spend the remaining budget where real evidence indicates the strongest expected incremental ticket return.

## Budget

| Phase | Purpose | Planned spend |
|---|---|---:|
| Baseline | Observe Resolution sales with no paid media | EUR 0 |
| Wave 1 | Geography + creative | EUR 90 |
| Wave 2 | Age refinement on winners | EUR 80 |
| Search | High-intent demand | EUR 40 |
| Retargeting | Warm audience if pool is large enough | EUR 30 |
| Scale reserve | Best validated cell(s) | EUR 260 |
| **Total** |  | **EUR 500** |

## Phase 0 — no-paid baseline

When ticket sales are live, collect approximately 48 hours of seat movement before paid ads if timing permits. This gives a local Resolution sales-velocity reference.

If the launch timetable makes a clean baseline impossible, mark that limitation in the campaign lab; do not silently infer it.

## Wave 1 — geography + creative

Broad age 20–60 to avoid fragmenting a small budget.

Choose the target market city in the dashboard. Wave 1 then uses three balanced zones from that city. The default Garching / Munich North preset uses ESO / Forschungszentrum, Garching / Hochbrück, and Universität / Schwabing. Other presets generate their own three local zones; custom city mode uses a neutral centre/north/south layout until real performance data exists.

Test two creatives:

- SXSW / award-proof angle
- Music + 360° immersive-experience angle

Six Meta cells at EUR 15 = EUR 90.

Primary early metrics: qualified landing-page visits, CTR, video completion, and verified purchases if available.

## Wave 2 — age refinement

Only the two best Wave 1 geographies advance. Use the winning creative and split:

- 20–34
- 35–60

Four cells at EUR 20 = EUR 80.

This ordering avoids creating 12 weak cells on day one.

## Search-intent test

EUR 40 on tightly controlled high-intent search terms related to ESO, Garching, planetarium/fulldome, Resolution, immersive music experiences, and relevant film/artist terms.

Use exact/phrase-like intent where practical; avoid broad generic entertainment traffic that cannot be evaluated with a EUR 500 budget.

## Retargeting

EUR 30 only if there is a sufficiently large warm audience. If not, leave that money unspent or roll it into the scale reserve.

## EUR 260 scale decision

Scale only after evaluating:

- controlled incremental CPA where available, or tracked-purchase CPA clearly labelled as non-causal;
- landing-page efficiency when purchase attribution is unavailable;
- Resolution's sales pace relative to ESO empirical baseline;
- sample size and model confidence;
- whether performance is stable rather than driven by one anomalous observation.

A poor experiment is a valid result. The system should recommend not scaling rather than force allocation.

## Attribution hierarchy

- **A:** ESO purchase source report.
- **B:** unique source or promo code.
- **C:** platform sale event reliably tied to ESO checkout.
- **D:** controlled holdout or staggered rollout that estimates incremental sales.
- **E:** aggregate ESO seat movement only.

A–C count tracked purchases by source but do not, alone, prove incrementality. D can support an incremental estimate if its control is valid. E cannot identify the ticket return of a particular geography, channel, or creative.

## Creative rules

Keep all other major variables constant within a creative test. Do not change audience, geography, budget, copy, and video simultaneously if the purpose is to learn the creative effect.

## Decision metrics

Configured project targets:

- success occupancy reference: 60%
- target incremental tickets from marketing: 40
- maximum desired incremental CPA: EUR 10

These are decision targets, not guaranteed outcomes.
